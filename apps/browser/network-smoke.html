Experimental Tor networking. HTTPS through Tor is TLS 1.3 with certificate checks, and the Tor directory must carry a quorum of pinned authority signatures. Anonymity is weaker than Tor Browser. Some sites block Tor exits.

No requests run until you press a button. Do not enter credentials or private repository URLs.
